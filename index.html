My Love Letter To You

My Dearest Husband, Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️ As I sit down to write this letter, my heart is filled with gratitude for the beautiful journey we have shared together. Sometimes I look back and wonder how fate brought us together in February 2020, and how, by 15 June 2020, our hearts had already found a home in each other. From that day until now, we have created countless memories together. We have laughed until our stomachs hurt, shared dreams about our future, stood by each other during difficult times, and yes, we have had our share of arguments and misunderstandings too. But every fight taught us something valuable. It showed us that our love is stronger than our differences and that no matter how far apart we feel, we always find our way back to each other. The last seven months have been one of the hardest chapters of our journey. Having you in London while I am here has made me realise how much of my happiness is connected to you. I miss the little things the most: your presence, your voice beside me, our conversations, and the comfort of knowing you are just a touch away. Distance has tested us, but it has never weakened my love for you. Instead, it has made me cherish you even more. You are not only my husband but also my best friend, my safe place, and the person who understands me like no one else. Thank you for loving me through all my moods, my imperfections, and every version of myself. Thank you for choosing us, every single day. On your birthday, I want you to know that no matter how many miles separate us, my heart is always with you. I am proud of the life we are building together and excited for all the birthdays, adventures, and memories that still await us. May this year bring you happiness, success, good health, and everything your heart desires. And most importantly, may it bring us closer to the day when we no longer have to count the distance between us. I love you more today than I did yesterday, and I know I will love you even more tomorrow. Happy Birthday, my forever person. With all my love, Your Wife ❤️
